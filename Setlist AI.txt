Brainstorming: Setlist AI Context & Explanations
This document outlines ideas for adding context, educational content, and use-case examples to the Setlist AI application, helping users understand the "magic" behind the setlist generation.
1. What is Setlist AI? (The "Elevator Pitch")
* Concept: Your personal AI DJ that doesn't just shuffle songs, but curates a journey.
* Tagline: "Stop Shuffling. Start Performing." or "Turn your Playlist into a Performance."
* Value Prop: takes the chaos of a random Spotify shuffle and applies professional DJ rules—harmonic mixing, energy flow, and beat matching—to create a seamless listening experience.
2. The Logic (The "Magic" Explained)
Demystifying the algorithm to build trust and authority.
A. The "Vibe" Curve (Energy Flow)
* The Problem: Random shuffle is a roller coaster. You get a banger followed by a ballad, killing the momentum.
* The Solution: Setlist AI uses a "Mountain" Energy Curve.
   * The Warm-up (0% - 30%): Starts with lower energy tracks to set the mood and let people settle in.
   * The Climb (30% - 75%): Gradually increases intensity, building anticipation.
   * The Peak (75%): hits the highest energy tracks when the vibe is right.
   * The Cooldown (75% - 100%): Gently brings the energy back down for a smooth finish.
B. Harmonic Mixing (Camelot Wheel)
* The Problem: Some songs just sound "wrong" together, even if the beat is right. Their musical keys clash (dissonance).
* The Solution: We use the Camelot Wheel system used by pro DJs.
   * Compatible Keys: The engine only picks next songs that are harmonically compatible (e.g., 8A → 8A, 8A → 9A, or 8A → 8B).
   * The Result: Transitions feel "musical" and seamless, like one long continuous track.
C. Beat Matching (BPM Protection)
* The Logic: We ensure no song jumps more than +/- 5 BPM from the previous one. This keeps the rhythm steady so you don't lose the groove.
3. Use Cases (Who is this for?)
🎧 The Bedroom DJ / Aspiring Producer
* Scenario: You want to record a mix but don't want to spend 3 hours auditing your library.
* Benefit: Instant crate-digging. Get a shortlist of 30 perfecly compatible songs to load into your DJ software and start mixing immediately.
🏠 The Party Host
* Scenario: You're hosting a dinner party that turns into a dance party.
* Benefit: A setlist that "reads the room" for you. It starts chill while people eat/chat, then builds up energy automatically as the night progresses. No "passing the aux" required.
🏃 The Runner / Workout
* Scenario: You need a playlist that paces you.
* Benefit: A structure that mirrors a workout: warm-up, high-intensity intervals (peak), and cool-down stretch.
📻 The Curator / Radio Show
* Scenario: You make playlists for a blog, radio show, or brand.
* Benefit: Create a "professional" sounding flow that keeps listeners engaged longer because the transitions aren't jarring.

* Visualizer: A simple line graph showing the Energy Curve of the generated setlist vs. the random input.
* Camelot Wheel Graphic: Visual helper showing the path the setlist takes around the wheel.
