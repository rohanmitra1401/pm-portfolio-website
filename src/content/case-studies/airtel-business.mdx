## Context

Airtel's enterprise ops teams logged B2B orders manually — a 12-hour process per cycle, error-prone, unscalable. Separately, 3,000 enterprise customers sat on legacy systems that made validation and service delivery slow.

## What I did

- Built and scaled a **B2B SaaS Order Entry platform** for internal ops teams, automating the manual logging workflow: **75% time reduction, 12 hours → 3 hours**
- Drove the migration of **3,000 enterprise customers** from legacy systems to the **LEO platform**: validation TAT **down 80%**, service delivery timelines **down 30%**

## Why it matters in my arc

This is where I learned enterprise-grade product discipline — internal tools, migration sequencing, and ops stakeholders — before applying it to consumer scale at PhonePe.

**Skills:** B2B SaaS · internal tooling · legacy migration · process automation
